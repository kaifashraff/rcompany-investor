Salary ka sach — company ko nuksaan nahi

Kaif ko 20% equity ke saath ek basic salary milegi. Ye sunkar lagta hai “company do jagah se degi” — aisa nahi hai. Samjhiye:

Salary = company ka kharcha (expense), jaise dukaan ka kiraya aur staff ki tankhwaah.
  • Equity (20%) = munafe me hissa. Munafa hoga tabhi milega — company pe bojh nahi.
  • Basic salary = roz kaam karne ki tankhwaah. Ye profit se pehle, kharche me likhi jaati hai — har company apne working partner/manager ko deti hai.
  • Example: mahine me 10 lakh bikri, 2 lakh kharcha (jisme Kaif ki salary bhi), to 8 lakh par munafa batega — Kaif ko usme se 20%. Salary lene se company ka munafa kam nahi hota, hisaab saaf rehta hai.
  • Isse Kaif roz dukaan pe tikke rehne ke liye accountable rehta hai — salary kaam ke badle, equity nateeje ke badle.
